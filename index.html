How the work runs

Four steps, in this order, every time. The point of the sequence is that you always know what is being built and why.

01

Scope in writing

What the system must do, what it must not do, and how we will both know it works. Ambiguity gets resolved here, not mid-build.

02

Build in the open

Progress you can look at, tests written alongside the code, and numbers reported honestly — including the ones that look bad.

03

Handover

Source code, a written handover guide and a walkthrough. You end up able to run and change it without me.

04

Support that ends

A short support window after delivery. After that, changes are a new piece of work rather than a standing subscription.
